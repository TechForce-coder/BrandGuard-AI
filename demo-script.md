# Demo script (about 5 minutes)

**Before you start:** open the live app in two browser profiles (one for the creator, one for the manager). Check `/api/health`. Prepare a draft text with planted problems, for example a superlative claim ("the best in the world, guaranteed results"), a made-up discount, and an image with alt-text "image". Keep a screen recording as a backup. Render may be asleep, so open the app a few minutes early.

| Time | Who | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Presenter | Show the login page | "Brands lose control when many people publish. BrandGuard checks content before it goes out, and people stay in charge." |
| 0:30 | Manager (profile 2) | Log in with the demo card, open **Rulebook** and **Facts** | "The manager keeps the brand rules and the approved facts, each fact with an owner and a valid-until date." |
| 1:00 | Creator (profile 1) | Log in, **New draft**, paste the prepared text, add the image alt-text, save | "The creator writes in one place and sees problems before sending it for approval." |
| 1:30 | Creator | Click **Scan** | "Plain-code checks run first, then Gemini checks against our rules and facts." |
| 2:00 | Creator | Open one finding | "Each finding quotes the exact text, explains why, and suggests a small fix. The quote is checked against the draft so the AI cannot invent text." |
| 2:30 | Creator | Accept one, reject one, dispute one with a reason | "Every finding needs a human decision. A dispute needs a reason." |
| 3:00 | Creator | Try to submit with one finding open, then resolve it and submit | "Submission is blocked until everything is resolved." |
| 3:30 | Manager | Open **Review**, show the diff, add a comment, **Approve** | "The manager sees findings and decisions, and can approve or request changes. A manager cannot approve their own draft." |
| 4:00 | Manager | Open **Audit** | "Every step is recorded: who did what, and when." |
| 4:20 | Manager | Open **Dashboard** | "A live view of approvals and activity." |
| 4:40 | Presenter | Show the image check result | "Images are checked for alt-text and reviewed by Gemini." |
| 5:00 | Presenter | Close with limits and roadmap | "Today: text and images with alt-text. Next: expired-fact flags, palette and logo checks, and retrieval for large rulebooks." |

## If something fails

- **Scan slow or error:** say the free Gemini quota may be busy, show the plain-code findings and the warning; they are labelled partial.
- **Backend asleep:** wait about a minute and refresh.
- **Login fails:** use the second demo account, or play the recording.
